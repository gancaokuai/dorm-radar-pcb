# Radar Device Enclosures

SolidWorks 2024 editable enclosure models for the TX and RX boards.

## TX Enclosure

- Outer dimensions: 77 mm x 61 mm x 24 mm
- PCB: 70 mm x 42 mm
- Four M2.5 mounting positions
- ESP32-C3 antenna opening
- Openings for battery, LD2450 and programming connectors

## RX Enclosure

- Outer dimensions: 75 mm x 61 mm x 24 mm
- PCB: 68 mm x 42 mm
- Four M2.5 mounting positions
- ESP32-C3 antenna opening
- Battery/programming cable openings
- 4 mm LED window

## Printing

- Material: PETG, ABS or PLA
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Perimeters: 3 to 4
- Infill: 20 to 30 percent
- Print the base with its floor on the build plate
- Print the cover flat
- Use M2.5 x 8 mm self-tapping screws, four per enclosure

The ESP32 antenna area must remain free of metal. Use a non-metallic enclosure only.

## Print Files

Each device folder contains:

- Base STL
- Cover STL
- Combined print-plate STL with base and cover pre-arranged
- SolidWorks SLDPRT/SLDASM source
- STEP exchange files

All STL files were verified as closed manifold meshes. The print plate files are ready to import directly into a slicer.

## Fastening

Recommended removable assembly, per device:

- 4 x M2.5 x 22 mm cross-head self-tapping screws
- 4 x M2.5 x 15 mm nylon through-spacers
- 4 x M2.5 nylon washers
- 4 x self-adhesive rubber feet

Alternative tape assembly:

- 4 x M2.5 x 5 mm self-tapping screws for the PCB-to-boss mounting
- 3M VHB or equivalent double-sided tape for the cover
